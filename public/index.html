    How to play

    Drag a piece, or tap it and then the square. The right mouse button draws arrows and marks squares.

    • KingOne square in any direction. It can never move into check.
    • QueenThe strongest: moves straight or diagonally, as far as it likes.
    • RookMoves straight, horizontally or vertically, as far as it likes.
    • BishopMoves diagonally, as far as it likes.
    • KnightMoves in an "L" (two squares and one to the side). It can jump over pieces.
    • PawnMoves one square forward (two on its first move) and captures diagonally. On the last rank it promotes to a queen, rook, bishop or knight.
